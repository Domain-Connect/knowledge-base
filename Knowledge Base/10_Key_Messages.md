## Core Soundbites

### The problem
> "Half of the people who register a domain and sign up for a service give up before they can actually use it — because DNS configuration is too complex. These aren't casual visitors. They're paying customers. We're losing them at the finish line."

> "DNS configuration is where the domain industry loses its customers. We've spent decades perfecting registration. We left the last step broken."

> "Microsoft maintains 16 help sites — 10 of them registrar-specific — just to help people configure DNS for email. That's not a user problem. That's a structural problem with how the industry works."

### The solution
> "Domain Connect replaces 40 minutes of manual DNS entry with one click. That's not an improvement in user experience. That's a different category of experience."

> "Domain Connect makes DNS configuration invisible. The user enters their domain, clicks Connect, logs into their DNS provider, approves — and it's done. No DNS knowledge required."

> "Domain Connect is what DNS configuration looks like when it's designed for humans."

### The trust model
> "Domain Connect doesn't give services free access to your DNS. Your DNS provider reviews every template before it can be used. You approve every change. You can cancel at any time."

> "The key innovation in Domain Connect isn't automation — it's controlled automation. Service providers define what they need. DNS providers vouch for it. Users approve it. No one skips any of those steps."

### The scale
> "35% of the .com zone is already covered by DNS providers that support Domain Connect. 720 templates from 408 service providers are deployed. This isn't an experiment anymore — it's infrastructure."

> "Domain Connect has been running in production since 2016. The IETF is now standardizing it. The question for the rest of the industry isn't whether to support it — it's how quickly."

### The business case for registries and registrars
> "A domain in active use renews at 90%. An unused domain renews at 70%. DNS configuration failure is one of the biggest barriers between registration and use. Domain Connect is the most direct fix available."

> "DNS configuration support tickets drop when you implement Domain Connect. Renewal rates go up. Customer satisfaction improves. It's the rare infrastructure investment that pays for itself."
